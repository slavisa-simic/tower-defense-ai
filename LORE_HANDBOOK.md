# Border Defenders lore handbook

Updated Phase 6B, 2026-11-02. LORE_BIBLE.md owns the current premise. Historical phase records remain provenance.

## Premise

A Serbian military quarantine line holds the Danube riverbank against mutated amphibious organisms. Their origin remains mysterious: industrial contamination, radiation, abandoned research and deep-water disturbance leave conflicting evidence. Direct emergence, drifting organic cysts and living brood carriers bring the contaminated ecosystem ashore. The river remains left and the defended inland line right.

## Environmental storytelling

Damp silt, muddy tracks, weathered concrete, patchwork repairs, dull olive canvas, rusted metal and scattered supply debris suggest a long-maintained defensive line under strain. Fortifications use practical sandbags, concrete walls, small observation roofs, generators, radio/radar equipment and supply crates. Abandoned equipment and damaged logistics supplies reinforce the quarantine setting. Restrained quarantine warning markings and utilitarian local military equipment establish setting without fantasy ornament, futuristic neon or cartoon proportions.

Keep the battlefield readable: sparse props on terrain, quiet open combat lanes, strong base silhouette. Signs viewed directly overhead show their top edge/support or a ground-facing marker; never tilt the camera to show a front-facing placard. Fences, antennae and floodlights must also use true overhead footprints. A spotlight is a compact directional searchlight housing; a floodlight is a broader twin-lamp support head.

## Scope boundary

Decorative fences, gates and machinery do not own gameplay. Phase 6B biological arrivals reuse the existing assault scheduler and EnemySpawner. Existing gameplay identifiers using sea/ocean/coast remain intact. New systems beyond the explicit phase remain separately scoped.
