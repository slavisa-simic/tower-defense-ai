# Border Defenders lore bible

Phase 3A-1, 2026-09-18. Visual fiction for the user's alternate post-nuclear Balkan timeline; not a claim about real events or people.

## Premise

A Serbian military quarantine line holds the Danube riverbank against infected incursions from southern radiation zones. Radiation-resistant inflatable boats and improvised river craft carry the visual story of repeated crossings. The left edge of the playable field represents the river; the defended base is on the right. Geographic references do not rotate the map.

## Environmental storytelling

Damp silt, muddy tracks, weathered concrete, patchwork repairs, dull olive canvas, rusted metal and scattered supply debris suggest a long-maintained defensive line under strain. Fortifications use practical sandbags, concrete walls, small observation roofs, generators, radio/radar equipment and supply crates. Craft may be intact, torn or abandoned. Restrained quarantine warning markings and utilitarian local military equipment establish setting without fantasy ornament, futuristic neon or cartoon proportions.

Keep the battlefield readable: sparse props on terrain, quiet open combat lanes, strong base silhouette. Signs viewed directly overhead show their top edge/support or a ground-facing marker; never tilt the camera to show a front-facing placard. Fences, antennae and floodlights must also use true overhead footprints. A spotlight is a compact directional searchlight housing; a floodlight is a broader twin-lamp support head.

## Scope boundary

Boats, fences, gates, towers and machinery in this art phase are visual objects. They add no transport, disembarkation, collision, route blocking, power, detection or new combat rules. Reference enemy/defense silhouettes are not production units or animation sheets. Existing gameplay identifiers using sea/ocean/coast remain intact. Full enemy production and initial animation sheets are proposed for Phase 3A-2 after this foundation passes review.
