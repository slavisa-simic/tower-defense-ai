# Border Defenders — Danube quarantine

The river is alive with things that should not be able to survive there. A catastrophic change in the Danube ecosystem has produced aggressive amphibious organisms. The quarantine line protects inland Serbia and its population centers from the contaminated river's expanding ecology.

The origin remains unresolved. Industrial effluent, radiation, disturbed deep-water habitats and abandoned research or military sites leave conflicting traces. No confirmed explanation, named human faction or supernatural cause is established. Environmental evidence should sustain the mystery.

Organisms share gills, sensory pits, membranes, keratin armor and sediment-stained tissue. Crawlers form the common shoreline population; Skitters exploit gaps; Bulwarks carry old riverbed armor; Spitters secrete corrosive material; Broodlings gather in dense larval swarms; Burrowers travel briefly beneath soft ground. Swarm, Armored and Aberrant strains describe biological patterns, not human allegiances.

The Danube delivers assaults through disturbed-water emergence, drifting cysts, living brood organisms and immense Leviathan carriers. The Dredger is a sediment-armored bottom predator. The Broodmother's reproductive chambers release bounded groups of larvae. These names and designs are original to this project.

HOMICIDES estimates projected civilian casualties when an organism crosses the final quarantine line and escapes toward populated areas. Crossing commits one breach, pays no kill reward and removes the creature. Military defenses, RSD logistics, checkpoints and industrial docks remain grounded in the existing setting.

Historical phase reports and archived assets describe superseded production decisions. They are provenance, not current lore. Phase 6C is not begun by this document.
